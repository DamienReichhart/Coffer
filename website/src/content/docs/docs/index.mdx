**7zip-GUI** is a free, open-source desktop app for working with archives on Windows, macOS and
Linux. It gives the 7-Zip command-line tool a modern, native window: browse an archive like a
folder, extract all of it or just what you selected, create archives with every option 7-Zip
offers, test them and compute checksums — without typing a single switch.

<DocScreenshot name="welcome" alt="The 7zip-GUI welcome screen with its drop zone and recent archives" />

## What it is — and what it isn’t

- **A front end for 7-Zip.** Every archive is read and written by the official 7-Zip command-line
  program (`7zz`, `7z` or `7za`) on your computer. 7zip-GUI builds the command line, runs it, reads
  its progress and shows you the result.
- **Not a copy of 7-Zip.** 7zip-GUI does not include, download or install 7-Zip. You install
  7-Zip yourself, once, from its official source or your system’s package manager — see
  [Installing 7-Zip](/docs/installing-7zip/).
- **Independent.** 7zip-GUI is not affiliated with or endorsed by 7-Zip or its author, Igor Pavlov.

## Highlights

- Browse archives without extracting them: folders, breadcrumbs, sorting, search and archive
  properties.
- Extract everything or a selection, with or without folder paths, and choose what happens to
  files that already exist.
- Create 7z, ZIP, TAR, GZIP, BZIP2, XZ and WIM archives with full control over the level, method,
  dictionary, solid blocks and threads.
- AES-256 encryption, including encrypted file names in 7z archives.
- Split archives into volumes, exclude files by pattern, delete originals after compressing.
- Test archive integrity and compute CRC32, CRC64, SHA-1, SHA-256 and BLAKE2sp checksums.
- A job queue with progress, cancel and logs.
- Light and dark themes, drag and drop, and signed automatic updates.
